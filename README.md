# Callship: The Low-Pressure, ADHD-Friendly Friendship & Orbit CRM
> *"Never let a friendship fade out of sight. Reconnect without the phone guilt."*

## 🌟 Executive Summary & Elevator Pitch

**Callship** is an intentional, anti-anxiety relational companion designed for neurodivergent thinkers, introverts, and busy humans who genuinely love their friends but struggle with "out of sight, out of mind" object permanence and text paralysis.

Unlike rigid enterprise CRMs that treat personal connections like business sales leads, or pushy habit trackers that induce guilt with menacing red badges, **Callship** transforms friendship maintenance into a peaceful, cosmic ritual. It features low-friction 2-to-5-minute phone sprints, shame-free reset loops, ambient body doubling, conversational icebreakers, and an escalating gamification system that celebrates every micro-effort of human warmth.

---

## 🎯 Core Value Proposition & The "Why"

### The Problem
- **ADHD & Friendship Object Permanence:** "If I don't see someone every day, my brain forgets to reach out—not because I don't care, but because executive dysfunction gets in the way."
- **The Guilt Spiral:** Days turn into weeks, weeks turn into months. When you finally remember, reaching out feels awkward, heavy, and full of shame.
- **The "Overwhelm" of Traditional CRMs:** Spreadsheets, infinite to-do lists, and rigid deadline trackers create pressure and demand energy that introverted and ADHD brains don't have.
- **Phone Anxiety:** Not knowing what to say or fearing a 90-minute call when you only have 5 minutes of social battery.

### The Callship Solution
1. **Single-Card Focus (Tinder-for-Staying-in-Touch):** Instead of staring at an overwhelming list of 50 people you've neglected, you see **one friendly face** at a time with a single gentle nudge.
2. **Shame-Free Reset (Self-Compassion Mechanic):** If you've ghosted someone for 6 months, one click wipes away all overdue flags and grants you +20 XP for choosing kindness over self-criticism.
3. **Structured Micro-Calls (2-to-5 Minute Sprints):** Built-in timers with pre-call breathing guides and scripted exit lines ("I've only got 3 minutes before my kettle boils, just wanted to hear your voice!") take away the fear of endless phone traps.
4. **Adaptive Social Battery Modes:** Low on energy? The app auto-filters your daily shuffle to only show low-spoon friends or suggests sending a lightweight 10-second meme instead of a call.
5. **Cosmic Orbital Map:** Visualizes your relationship circles (Inner Core, Close Circle, Outer Orbit, Distant Constellation) as an interactive solar system.
